Cycle Phase Guide
How each phase affects scores, energy, cravings, sleep, and activities. Cycle expanded to 35 days max. Menstrual phase 1–6 days.
ℹ️Add Cycle Day (Number, max 35) to your Hub Bio Log. Type today's day number — Day 1 = first day of bleeding. The calculator uses this to adjust all scores automatically. Also add to Blood Pressure database to track hormonal BP patterns.
🔴 Menstrual Phase — Day 1–6
HRV impact
Low days 1–3 (prostaglandins + blood loss). Rising by day 5–6. Do not compare to follicular baseline.
Energy
Low to moderate. Iron depleting days 1–4. Fatigue is physiological — rest is appropriate, not weakness.
Cravings
HIGH. Carbs, chocolate, salty foods. Prostaglandins drive this. Redirect: dark chocolate + protein shake, sweet potato, electrolyte drink for salt cravings.
Score adjust
−5 to −8pts from Warrior Score. Zone 1 behavior appropriate at 75+.
Best activities
Crochet, diamond painting, comfort dramas, heating pad, gentle walks. Days 1–2: minimize standing household tasks.
Nutrition
Iron-rich foods days 1–5: red meat, spinach, lentils. Pre-plan one higher-carb meal — track it, don't restrict. Protein shakes easier than cooking on cramp days.
🔵 Follicular Phase — Day 7–13
HRV impact
Rising steadily. Estrogen anti-inflammatory and vasodilatory. Best learning and performance window of the month.
Energy
Rising. Best phase for new habits, new patterns, complex projects, deep cleaning.
Cravings
Lowest. Easiest phase for hitting protein target and staying under sodium limit.
Score adjust
Standard thresholds — may exceed usual baseline.
Best activities
New crochet techniques, complex diamond painting canvases, new Asian drama series, deep cleaning oven/air fryer, full meal prep batches.
Nutrition
Stock protocol-friendly foods now. Set up the next (luteal) phase. Use this energy window strategically.
✅ Ovulatory Phase — Day 14–20
HRV impact
Peak HRV. Estrogen + testosterone surge. Highest Warrior Scores happen here.
Energy
Peak physical and cognitive energy. Best window for demanding work and exercise.
Cravings
Moderate. Appetite may suppress — set meal reminders. Risk is undereating protein.
Score adjust
Standard or above. Your true performance ceiling.
Best activities
Full meal prep, oven/air fryer deep clean, reorganizing spaces, complex crochet patterns, challenging diamond painting projects.
Nutrition
Set meal alarms — appetite suppression means protein slips. Resistance training most effective this phase for body recomposition.
💜 Luteal Phase — Day 21–35
HRV impact
Expect 10–20% lower than follicular baseline. Progesterone raises resting HR and reduces HRV. Normal — not protocol failure. ADHD symptoms often amplify (dopamine fluctuates with estrogen).
Energy
Declining especially days 27–35. Brain fog is real. Working memory impairment amplifies. Executive function decreases.
Cravings
HIGH and rising, peaks days 28–35. Serotonin drops → carb cravings = brain trying to self-regulate. Redirect: sweet potato, rice, fruit with protein, dark chocolate. Don't fight — redirect.
Score adjust
−10 to −15pts from all thresholds. Zone 1 behavior appropriate at Warrior Score 75+. Do NOT compare to ovulatory phase scores.
Best activities
Diamond painting (repetitive, satisfying), simple crochet rows, comfort dramas and pro wrestling, laundry, simple one-task household items.
Nutrition
Pre-plan: sweet potato, rice, fruit with protein, dark chocolate 70%+. This is a maintenance phase, not a performance phase. Reducing expectations is appropriate, not failure.
